# Upsell — spec v1

Cross-sell and upgrade prompts across surfaces.

## Rules

- **UPSELL-R-101** — An upsell is never shown to an account that already holds the entitlement
  being upsold.
- **UPSELL-R-102** — Upsell prompts on the Services page deep-link to the purchase channel the
  user originally bought in.
- **UPSELL-R-201** — A retail-activated subscription may be upsold to a paid plan, and the
  remaining retail term is forfeited at conversion.
